How to Play // Tactical Guide

              
            


            

              

                
1

                

                  
Protect the Core ⚡

                  

                    Hostiles march along the illuminated pathway toward your central Energy Core. Each enemy that
                    breaches drains Core energy. Don't let health reach zero!
                  

                

              


              

                
2

                

                  
Free Placement Defense ◈

                  

                    Click + Build Tower (or press keys 1 - 8) to select a tower, then
                    click anywhere on the arena grid outside the enemy path to construct.
                  

                

              


              

                
3

                

                  
Upgrades & Target Priority ⛭

                  

                    Click any built tower on the map to open its inspector: Upgrade [U] for higher
                    damage/range, Sell [X] to refund credits, or toggle targeting priority (First,
                    Strongest, Closest).
                  

                

              


              

                
4

                

                  
Tactical Support Commands ⚡

                  

                    Trigger emergency abilities in swarms: [Q] EMP Blast (global stun),
                    [E] Air Strike (1,500 AoE bomb), or [R] Supercharge (+50% attack
                    rate).
